In computer science and other fields, many results are published first at conferences. Unlike journals, which have
the ISSN, conferences have no common identifier. Information about them (dates and places of the editions, the
series, the proceedings) is spread over publishers, bibliographies, library catalogues and registries. confref links
this information as open linked data and assigns each conference series and edition a stable identifier.

## History

confref was founded in 2013 by **Kai Eckert** and **Aliaksandr Birukou**.

- **2013–2015: Springer LOD.** Aliaksandr Birukou (Springer) and Kai Eckert (University of Mannheim) start to
  publish the metadata of Springer's conference proceedings as linked open data. lod.springer.com goes online in
  2014; the public launch follows in 2015, under CC0. dblp and TU Wien are the first data providers.
- **2015–2023: Stuttgart Media University.** The project moves with Kai Eckert to Stuttgart Media University. In 2018
  Springer Nature hands over the lod.springer.com data and the code of its conference portal, and Digital Science
  funds confref with a Catalyst Grant. portal.confref.org goes online in 2019 and lists about 38,000 conferences
  after the integration of dblp in 2020.
- **2019–2023: persistent identifiers.** confref takes part in the working group of Crossref and DataCite on
  persistent identifiers for conferences. From 2020, confref works with TIB Hannover on ConfIDent, a curated registry
  of academic events, and its data goes into ConfIDent. Results: *Persistent Identification for Conferences* (Data
  Science Journal 21, 2022, [doi:10.5334/dsj-2022-011](https://doi.org/10.5334/dsj-2022-011)). TIB and Kai Eckert
  applied jointly to the DFG for funding to continue this work; the proposal was not granted.
- **Since 2023: Technische Hochschule Mannheim.** With Kai Eckert's move to TH Mannheim, the portal was
  discontinued and the work concentrated on the knowledge graph. Since 2026 confref is being rebuilt as an open
  knowledge graph: each source a separate dataset, every version kept, every dataset generated by code in a public
  repository.

## Principles

- **Open:** all data CC0, unless a source requires otherwise; the dataset then states its licence.
- **Separate sources:** the statements of a source are kept apart from what confref derives from them.
- **Persistent:** identifiers are not reused; published versions are not changed.
- **Reproducible:** every dataset is generated by code in a public repository from documented inputs.
