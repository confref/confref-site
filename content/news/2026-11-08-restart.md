confref is being rebuilt in the [Research Group Prof. Dr. Kai Eckert](https://www.kaiec.de/) at the
[Technische Hochschule Mannheim](https://www.th-mannheim.de/) as an open knowledge graph of academic conferences.

The first datasets contain the data of the earlier project:

- the lod.springer.com dump of 2018: 1,764 conference series, 9,327 conferences, 59,684 books and 1.25 million
  chapters, CC0;
- the first Springer release of 2015;
- the 37,945 conferences of portal.confref.org (2020).

Further datasets cover dblp, Wikidata, ConfIDent and the GND.

Next steps: links between these datasets and a first set of confref identifiers for conference series and editions.
Downloads will be published on this site, a SPARQL endpoint at labs.confref.org.
