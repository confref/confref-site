## Privacy

This website is a static site. It has no contact form, no user accounts and no login, sets no cookies and does not
process data you enter.

**Server logs.** When you open a page, your browser transmits data that is recorded in the server log, including your
IP address, the requested page and the time of the request. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure.

**No tracking, no external resources.** The site uses no analytics. All stylesheets, fonts and images are served from
this site; no data is transmitted to third parties when you open a page.
