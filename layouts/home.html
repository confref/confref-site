Open identifiers for academic conferences

confref links open data on conference series and their editions from several sources and assigns each series and edition a stable identifier. The data is CC0.

Method

Sources, links, identifiers

Sources

Each source is a separate dataset with its own licence: dblp, Wikidata, ConfIDent, the GND and the Springer LOD data.

Links

Records of the same series or edition are linked across the sources. Each set of links states its method and its measured quality.

Identifiers

Each series and edition receives a confref identifier with a check character, such as s10000y. Identifiers are not reused.

Since 2013

History

confref started with Springer's conference proceedings as linked open data and continued as the confref portal. Since 2023 the work concentrates on the knowledge graph, now built at the Technische Hochschule Mannheim.

Read the history

  1. 2013Start of the Springer LOD pilot at the University of Mannheim
  2. 2015Public launch of lod.springer.com, CC0
  3. 2018Digital Science Catalyst Grant for confref
  4. 2019portal.confref.org online
  5. 2020Cooperation with TIB on ConfIDent
  6. 2023Focus on the knowledge graph at TH Mannheim

confref is a project of the Research Group Prof. Dr. Kai Eckert at the Technische Hochschule Mannheim (Mannheim Technical University).
